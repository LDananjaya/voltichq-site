Privacy Policy — Word Search Explorer

Last updated: August 26, 2026
Draft — written for the app's current local-only build. Confirm this still matches before submitting for closed testing, and update it if you add sign-in, ads, or analytics later.

Word Search Explorer ("the App") is developed by Lahiru Dananjaya ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the App collects and how it's used.

No account, no personal data collected

The App works fully offline and doesn't require you to create an account or sign in. We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal information.

What stays on your device

Your progress, completed puzzles, and settings are stored locally on your device only. This data is not sent to us or to any third party, and it's deleted automatically if you uninstall the App.

No advertising or analytics SDKs

The current version of the App does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, so no advertising identifier or usage data is collected.

Children

The App is a general-audience word puzzle game and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the App later adds features such as sign-in, cloud sync, purchases, or ads, this policy will be updated to describe them, with a revised "Last updated" date posted at this same URL.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to voltichq@gmail.com.
