About

Built by one QA-minded developer

VoltichQ is run by Lahiru Dananjaya, a QA consultant who spends his day job testing financial software before it ships to real users. The same discipline carries over here — every VoltichQ release goes through structured testing before it reaches Google Play, not just a quick playtest.

The goal isn't a huge catalog. It's a small, growing set of games that are simple to pick up, don't nag you with clutter, and actually work.
